Use Cases

Performance agencies, media operations

Many advertisers across many channels, approved, executed and rolled back from one console. ROAS diagnosis and budget reallocation included.

No OAuth, no developer token, no partner approval. The report file an agency already exports from the channel console is enough. A profile fills in currency, timezone, attribution window and source, each with its provenance, and the user maps only two columns, date and budget. The console is up on real advertiser data within five minutes.

What a profile does not know it does not fill in. VAT comes as a check to perform rather than a value, and a Naver conversion window arrives labeled as the sum of direct and indirect. Formats absent from official documentation are unsupported, and agency blogs are never used as a source.

A drop is investigated with evidence, and an axis that could not be seen is reported as unseen. Channel comparison refuses to total across different attribution bases. Plans are dry-run as a diff, only approved plans execute, and failure restores recorded values.
