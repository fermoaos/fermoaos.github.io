AgentOS

Edge agent

Intent is inferred on the device. With a 4-bit quantized SDK and federated learning, data never leaves the phone; the server only receives what was learned.

The SDK is 2.2MB, 42% of a 5MB budget. Intent vectorization finishes on-device at a p95 of 0.257ms. Raw utterances and profiles are never sent, only weights are aggregated, and all seven canaries went undetected. There is no ad request at all: the 3,000-item catalog ships as a bundle.

We do not say a 5MB SDK runs a three-billion-parameter model. The real structure has two tiers: an always-on path with a tiny static embedding we distilled ourselves, and deeper inference only on devices that ship an OS model. A model 220 times smaller reaches 78 to 87 percent of its teacher.

A privacy budget of ε ≤ 2.0 is a contract term, not a technical value. Pilots under 10,000 devices keep federated learning off and promise zero raw transfer. Above 10,000 it turns on and the campaign ε is published.
